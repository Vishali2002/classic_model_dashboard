
# Classic Models Power BI Dashboard
The Classic Models Sales Dashboard is designed to offer a comprehensive view of sales data across various product lines, regions, and time periods. It helps businesses identify top-performing products, understand market trends, and uncover factors influencing sales
This repository contains a Power BI dashboard that visualizes key sales and product data from the Classic Models dataset. The dashboard provides insights into product sales, customer distribution, and performance trends over the years.

# Top Selling Products:

1992 Ferrari 360 Spider (Red) is the best-selling product, generating $276.84K in sales.
Other top products include the 2001 Ferrari Enzo and the 1952 Alpine Renault.
Sales by Product Line:

Classic Cars dominate with 40.13% of total sales.
Vintage Cars and Motorcycles follow at 18.72% and 11.68%, respectively.
Sales Trends (2003-2005):

Total sales peaked in 2005, with $4.3M in revenue.
Monthly sales trends highlight significant seasonal variations.
Product Quantities Ordered:

Classic Cars lead in quantity with over 36K units sold, followed by Vintage Cars (23K units) and Motorcycles (13K units).
Regional Customer Distribution:

The dashboard includes a breakdown of customer numbers by country, providing a global view of sales distribution.
Profit Margins:

The overall profit margin stands at 39.84%, with total sales of $9.60M.
Influencing Factors:
Sales increase when products feature detailed descriptions, such as turnable front wheels, detailed interiors, and opening doors and trunks.
